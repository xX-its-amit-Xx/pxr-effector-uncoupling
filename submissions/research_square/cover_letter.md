To the Research Square editorial team,

I am submitting the manuscript "Cell-type-resolved decoupling of PXR target genes identifies hepatocyte-selective readouts for xenobiotic response" for posting on Research Square as a preprint.

A version of this work was previously declined by bioRxiv with a "student work" classification — not on scientific grounds, but on platform-level framing. I am submitting to Research Square because the platform's broader reproducibility-first scope is a much better fit for a single-author computational study whose principal contribution is a fully reproducible analysis pipeline with locked dependencies, continuous integration, a data dictionary, and per-gene evidence dossiers.

The study examines NR1I2 (PXR) target-gene coupling across 446,672 single human cells (10 cell types) from CELLxGENE Census, validates the finding across six orthogonal layers (parameter robustness, matched negative controls, GTEx bulk RNA-seq, direct rifamycin perturbation of primary human hepatocytes, LINCS L1000 cell-line response, and the Open Targets disease graph), and identifies a four-gene hepatocyte-selective biomarker panel (CYP2C8, CYP2C9, CYP3A5, ABCC2) for PXR pharmacodynamic readouts. The complete codebase, processed CSVs, figures, and analysis notebooks are hosted at https://github.com/xX-its-amit-Xx/pxr-effector-uncoupling under an MIT license, with a CITATION.cff and pinned environment.

By posting on Research Square I aim to make the work and the bundle discoverable while a peer-reviewed venue is selected. I confirm the work is original, not under consideration at a peer-reviewed journal, and the author has read and agrees to Research Square's posting terms.

Thank you for your consideration.

Sincerely,
Amit Shenoy
